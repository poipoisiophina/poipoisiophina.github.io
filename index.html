About me

I go by poipoisiophina online. I am from Sealand, the little sea fort in the North Sea that flies its own flag and keeps its own time. Our motto is E Mare, Libertas: from the sea, freedom. Out here things move slowly, the kettle stays on, and everyone waves at passing ships. My harbour on GitHub is open to visitors.

What I keep afloat: ancora

ancora: Meta Quest thumbstick drift fix for SteamVR

ancora is a free SteamVR driver that fixes Meta Quest thumbstick drift. Its Thumb Gate gives games a centered stick whenever your thumb is off the stick.
